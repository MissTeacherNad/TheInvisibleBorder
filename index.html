Expressing probability

Les modaux indiquent ici un degré de probabilité. Le temps de la déduction est porté par la forme du verbe qui suit.

MIGHTfaible
MAYpossible · 50%
MUSTtrès probable
WILLcertain

Past

modal + HAVE + past participle
Code words must have protected the network.

Present

modal + BE + V-ing
The patrols must be searching the area.

Future

MAY / WILL + verb
Their story will inspire others.

Passive voice

On choisit le passif lorsque le sujet grammatical subit l’action. L’agent n’est ajouté avec by que s’il apporte une information utile.

BE au temps voulu + past participle
Active: Federal officers arrested freedom seekers.
Passive: Freedom seekers were arrested by federal officers.
TempsForme passive
Presentare guided
Preteritewere guided
Present perfecthave been guided
Futurewill be guided

Vérification : garde le temps de la phrase active, accorde be avec le nouveau sujet et mets le verbe principal au participe passé.

The whole argument

Economic interest pushed slavery westward. A legal border divided territories, but federal law and slave catchers could cross that line. Freedom seekers therefore depended on timing, hidden knowledge, trusted people, safe places and code. The border shaped conflict and identity, but freedom required organised resistance.
